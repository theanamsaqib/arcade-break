ARCADE BREAK

Snake, Breakout and Sky Bird in one Chrome popup. Free, no ads, no tracking.

Download (free)

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera · open source

Three games

Snake

Classic. Arrow keys or WASD. Optional wrap-around walls.

Breakout

Smash the bricks. Keys or mouse. Gets faster every level.

Sky Bird

Tap to flap through the pipes. How far can you get?

Install in 1 minute

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin it with the puzzle-piece icon. Click to play, or press Ctrl+Shift+Y.

Privacy

Arcade Break asks for no permissions and makes no network requests. Best scores stay in your own browser.
